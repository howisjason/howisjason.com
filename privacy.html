Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Who I Am

I'm Jason Tang, and this is my website. I'm the person responsible for any information you share here. If you have a question about your information, email me at jason@howisjason.com.

When You Book a Call

The booking form asks for:

  • your name and email
  • your business name or links, if you add them
  • anyone you invite to the call
  • a reason, if you reschedule

Bookings run on Cal.com, and the call goes into my Google Calendar with a Google Meet link. I use what you give me to hold the call and to follow up about it. I don't sell it, and I don't share it beyond those two services.

When You Message Me

If you email me or message me on WhatsApp or Instagram, I get what you send and use it to reply. Those apps have their own privacy rules.

Counting Visits

I count visits to this site with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies, and it doesn't collect information that identifies you. If you came through a link a friend of mine shared, I can see which link it was, so I know who to thank.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security.

Cookies

The main page sets no cookies. The booking calendar comes from Cal.com, and loading it sets one security cookie, called __cf_bm, that helps keep out bots. It ends after 30 minutes of no activity.

Your Information, Your Choice

You can ask me to show you what I have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Just email jason@howisjason.com. I keep booking details only as long as we're talking or working together, and I delete them sooner if you ask.
