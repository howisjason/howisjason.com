# Where every picture in the kit comes from

Each file was downloaded once (18 Oct 2026) and resized here, so no page ever loads a picture
from someone else's server. One line per file. A new picture gets its line in the same commit.

- `enso.png`: the ensō, a round brush circle in grey ink (1200 x 1698, as served). Unsplash, by Diletta Davolio, https://images.unsplash.com/vector-1753390445088-6da20fb18f83 . Unsplash License.
- `plaster-tile.jpg`: the sunlit plaster wall (1280 x 560). The top 280px strip of the photo at 1280px wide, stacked over its own mirror image so it repeats down a page with no seam. Unsplash, by Pawel Czerwinski, https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1790513159876-28661b1e7441 . Unsplash License.
- `leaf-shadow.jpg`: plant shadow on a cream wall, with live green leaves at the top (1600 x 1066). Unsplash, by V. Strukovskaya, https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1573235670775-937074414ed4 . Unsplash License.
- `banana-leaf.jpg`: banana leaves and their shadow on a white wall (1104 x 1600). Unsplash, by Declan Sun, https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1732287322746-48f378f418e0 . Unsplash License.
- `calligraphy-profound-sincerity.jpg`: Jiun Onkō, "Profound Sincerity", ink calligraphy, the gold title's source (378 x 624, the Met's web size). The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access, https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/as/web-large/DP700745.jpg . Public domain, CC0.
